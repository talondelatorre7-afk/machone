The Directors

Talon & James

We don't make videos. We make art.

VITESSE was forged from a mutual obsession with automotive engineering and raw cinema. After collaborating on everything from a base model Alfa Romeo to a Ferrari SF90 XX Spider, we realized the automotive media industry was becoming entirely manufactured.

Our philosophy separates us from every other visual agency. We do not use shot lists. We do not write scripts. We never use AI. Every production is entirely sporadic by design. When you meticulously storyboard a vehicle, you lose its soul. We operate on instinct, reacting to the machine and the environment in real-time to capture untamed momentum. The result is an organic, uncompromising narrative that cannot be manufactured in a studio.

On set, the division of labor is absolute. James commands the pursuit vehicle, directs the set, and captures raw stills and BTS on a Canon 250D, while Talon locks in the heavy Canon R5 C on the tracking rig to pull the cinematic plates.

Hardware Specs

The Arsenal

A-Cam: Canon EOS R5 C

Our primary cinema weapon. Pushing heavy 10-bit XF-AVC files that offer massive dynamic range for aggressive, high-contrast color grading.

Pursuit: Tilta Hydra Alien Pro

Hard-mounted to the GMC Canyon chase vehicle, providing mechanical shock-absorption to isolate the camera from road vibration at highway speeds.

B-Cam: Canon 250D

Operated by James on set to capture high-fidelity BTS and secondary still photography while the primary tracking rig is engaged.

Action: GoPro Hero 13 Black

Deployed in high-risk zones and tight chassis mounts where traditional cinema glass cannot fit, capturing intense POV and mechanical action.

Texture: 2000s Digicam

An early 2000s Canon point-and-shoot utilized intentionally to cut nostalgic, raw, low-fi textures into our ultra-crisp 4K timelines.

Post: DaVinci Resolve

Where the art is finalized. We push our raw logs through a heavy-duty color pipeline, crushing the blacks and retaining highlights for a bespoke cinematic aesthetic.
