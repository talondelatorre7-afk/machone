The Mission

Bridging Machine and Media

VITESSE was founded to solve a critical issue in automotive media: capturing raw momentum without sacrificing cinematic fidelity. We don't just point a camera out the window—we bolt heavy metal to heavy metal, engineering visual experiences that match the precision of the machines we film.

  • 01

    High-Speed Tracking

    Vehicle-to-vehicle tracking utilizing mechanical damping systems to capture perfectly smooth, high-speed rollers and intense pursuit angles.

  • 02

    Commercial Media

    From static architectural lighting to dynamic motion, we craft uncompromising visual campaigns for hypercars, exotics, and bespoke builds.

  • 03

    Post-Production

    Rigorous editing and color grading pipelines utilizing DaVinci Resolve to extract maximum dynamic range and heavy, cinematic tones.
